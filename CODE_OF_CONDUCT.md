# Code of conduct

JudgeGauge is a technical community built around reproducible, careful measurement. Participation
requires respectful, evidence-led collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims and implementations, not people.
- State uncertainty and the limits of empirical results.
- Welcome contributors regardless of experience or background.
- Keep reviews specific, actionable, and proportional to the change.
- Protect credentials, private prompts, and sensitive evaluation data.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate credential exposure, and repeated bad-faith
disruption are not accepted. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict
participation when needed to protect the community.

For ordinary disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request. For sensitive conduct concerns,
contact the repository owner privately through the contact methods on the
[`DaBestCode` GitHub profile](https://github.com/DaBestCode). Do not disclose another person's private
information in a public issue.
